An original 3D open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: explore the coastal city of Solmar Bay on foot and by car, boat and motorcycle, run jobs for Vera Costa, outrun the Solmar PD under a five-star wanted system, and spend your earnings at Rico's Garage.

Play it at https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01 — serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop Chrome. No network needed: three.js is vendored under `game/vendor/`.

## The game

- **Open world**: a grid city with a beach, sea, marina, lighthouse point and airfield — drive it, walk it, steal it.
- **Missions and story**: six jobs for Rico Marlowe — First Wheels, Harbour Escape, City Taxi, Sunset Courier, Rooftop Leap, Lighthouse Cache — plus repeatable taxi fares.
- **Vehicles**: sedan, pickup, the "Coast Runner" motorcycle, a marina skiff and police interceptors.
- **Wanted system**: 1–5 stars, police cars that chase and ram, a helicopter at four stars, and a bust fine when the law catches you.
- **Progression**: cash buys garage repairs, an engine tune (+25% speed) and armor plating (+50 HP). Progress saves in your browser.
- **HUD**: wanted stars, compass, cash, health, speed, mission objectives and a minimap.
